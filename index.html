Statutory Regulator of the Accountancy Profession

As the statutory body established by the Government of Pakistan, ICAP regulates and advances the accountancy profession in the public interest. Through robust standard-setting, effective oversight, and disciplined enforcement, ICAP upholds the highest standards of ethics, transparency, and professional competence among Chartered Accountants.

ICAP Job Portal – Explore New Career Opportunities

The ICAP Job Portal, an initiative of MARCOM ICAP, is available through the secure Members' Area and features the latest career opportunities across diverse industries and sectors.

Access the Portal: https://member.icapoffice.org.pk

Advance your career by exploring new opportunities and professional openings tailored for ICAP members.

Employers & Members: If your organization has vacancies for qualified or partly qualified Chartered Accountants, share them with us at jobportal@icapoffice.org.pk to help create greater opportunities for the ICAP community.
